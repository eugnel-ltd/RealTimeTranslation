## Risks

- Provider events arrive after stop or restart: callbacks and continuations check their captured session and resource identity before changing state.
- A provider never acknowledges setup: a bounded timeout rejects startup and closes the connection; no microphone is acquired.
- Failed microphone or audio setup leaks a connection: startup catches dispose that session before propagating the error.
- Planned socket closure resembles failure: detach handlers and invalidate the old session before close; unexpected closure cleans resources and reports once.
- Translations return out of order: process socket messages in arrival order, with session checks after translation awaits.
- Real provider/device checks need credentials and microphone access: disclose these as unrun checks; do not call paid integrations, change secrets or deploy.
