Real-Time Translation

Speech transcription and translation with session templates and optional AI answers.

Access is currently restricted to the owner. The service is free and public sign-up is unavailable.

Follow speech across languages

The web app transcribes microphone speech and translates it into selected languages. It supports bilingual output so you can read the original text alongside a translation.

Tools for meetings and interviews

  • Session templates for meetings and different interview formats.
  • Optional AI question detection and answers, including typed questions.
  • Dark and light themes, keyboard shortcuts and optional browser transcript history.

Understand how your data is used

Speech and optional AI features use external providers. Read Data use and storage choices and the Privacy policy before recording. Consider other participants' privacy and obtain any necessary permission.
